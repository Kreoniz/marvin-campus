# Connected brow follow-up — 2026-10-20

This narrows the [earlier review](2026-10-20-mascot-copyright.md) to the selected
M2 body and the owner's preference for a connected brow. It concerns the
**2005 film Marvin**, a public nonprofit schedule app, and principally Russian
copyright law. It is a design risk assessment, not legal clearance.

**Finding:** a connected brow is not categorically forbidden. No reviewed rule
gives exclusive ownership of circles, mint, a sleepy expression or a unibrow as
abstract ingredients. Equally, round eyes or a redesigned torso do not establish
noninfringement. M2 could still reproduce protected character expression if the
remaining combination retains that character's recognizable identity; this
review does not establish that it legally does.

## Rules rechecked

- Civil Code art. 1259(5), (7) distinguishes unprotected ideas from objectively
  expressed, independently creative characters or parts. Art. 1270(2)(1), (9),
  (11) covers reproduction, adaptation and online availability, including use
  without profit. A small public audience supplies no general permission.
  [Official Rospatent text](https://rospatent.gov.ru/ru/documents/grazhdanskiy-kodeks-rossiyskoy-federacii-chast-chetvertaya).
- Supreme Court Plenum No. 10, paras. 80–82: character protection concerns its
  individualizing characteristics; incomplete matches and inessential changes
  may still reproduce a recognizable character. External similarity is only one
  consideration, and trademark confusion is a different inquiry. Para. 95 also
  recognizes that a similar but creatively independent work need not infringe;
  whether a work is an adaptation can require expert assessment.
  [Official court source](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/),
  [official Rospatent repost](https://rospatent.gov.ru/content/uploadfiles/ppvs-10-20190423.pdf).

## Observations and case-specific inference

I inspected M2, the current mint thinking illustration, the rejected E1 face,
and the [Henson production photograph](https://www.henson.com/jimsredbook/wp-content/uploads/2012/09/CShop_hhgg_grab_marvin5.jpg).
The [first-party archive article](https://www.henson.com/jimsredbook/2012/09/9131983/)
links the production design to the 2005 film; the photograph remains outside Git.

M2's charcoal torso, mint yoke, low shoulder caps and ivory neck are visible
departures from that film body. Its two-arm thinking pose is also different
from the inspected photograph. The larger residual resemblance is the face:
huge pale spherical forehead, small very low green eyes and a long sloping
shared divider. The film's eyes are pointed; M2's are rounded. These are visual
observations, not a finding of protected scope or infringement.

My inference is that varying the **placement, span and construction** of the
connected brow and eyes can improve differentiation while honoring the selected
body. Removing every connected brow is unnecessary as a categorical response.
No particular curve, eye shape or amount of moved detail guarantees clearance.
Head-only header/PWA assets require their own assessment: a changed body cannot
differentiate a head that is displayed without it.

The [asset record](../assets.md) and [retained prompts](../assets/mint-mascot-v1-prompts.txt)
document reference-based AI edits and deliberate continuity. Generation is not
proof of independent creation or permission; rights in the supplied cartoon
reference remain a separate unresolved question. The name Marvin with “Don't
Panic” and “42” reinforces a franchise reading in context, as a design inference.
No rightsholder chain, trademark registration, applicable licence, exception or
enforcement probability was established here.

## Face studies completed

Four transparent M2 edits were saved in the ignored preview directory
`.cache/mascot-connected-brow-2026-10-20/`; exact prompts and original output
locations are in `prompts.json`. No application asset was replaced.

| Study | Observed connected construction                                      |
| ----- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| U1    | Raised eyes beneath a shorter shallow curved shared lid.             |
| U2    | Shorter lids joined by a rounded raised central bridge.              |
| U3    | Compact level shared lid with closer eyes.                           |
| U4    | One continuous floating curved brow above separate small eye shapes. |

All four preserve the chosen body, crossing forearm and both hands. Each changes
the original long low diagonal facial arrangement; U4 changes the brow-to-eye
relationship most visibly. These are differentiation candidates, not legally
cleared alternatives. An assessment of any final public set must include the
actual head icons, full illustration, branding and reference provenance.
